Add music, then host it free

      
Your song, your monogram and one link that opens beautifully on every phone.

    


    

      

      
Why the music waits for a tap. iPhone Safari and Android Chrome strictly block websites from playing audio before a user taps the screen. The master prompts solve this natively: tapping “Open Invitation” on the 3D gate swings the doors open and starts your song at 50% volume, pausing automatically if the guest switches tabs or plays your invitation film.

    


    

      

        
Prepare your audio file

        
Pick a soft instrumental track (flute, santoor, rabab, veena, violin or an acoustic cover). Trim it to the best 60–90 seconds using mp3cut.net so the file stays under 2 MB for instant loading over WhatsApp.

      

      

        
Local folder setup

        
Rename the file to song.mp3 (and your monogram to monogram.png) and place it in the same folder as your index.html and config.js files. Verify audioSrc: "song.mp3" in config.js.

      

      

        
Testing audio inside AI previews

        
To hear your music inside Claude or Gemini Canvas before downloading, upload your MP3 to a direct-link host (such as catbox.moe or GitHub Raw) and paste the direct .mp3 URL into the prompt.
